Our Story

About Emily Maine Coons

Discover the world of majestic Maine Coon kittens at Emily Maine Coons. We're dedicated to breeding healthy, affectionate, and stunning Maine Coon cats that become beloved family members. Our kittens are raised with love and care, ensuring they grow into confident and playful companions.

Our Journey With Maine Coons

Our journey began with a deep passion for Maine Coon cats, known for their gentle nature, intelligence, and impressive size. Over the years, we've carefully selected champion bloodlines to produce kittens that embody the best traits of this magnificent breed. From the moment they're born, our kittens receive personalized attention, socialization, and the highest standards of care to prepare them for their forever homes.

At Emily Maine Coons, we believe in ethical breeding practices and transparency. Every kitten comes with health certifications, pedigree papers, and a lifetime of support. We're not just breeders; we're Maine Coon enthusiasts committed to matching the perfect kitten with loving families. Join us in celebrating the beauty and charm of Maine Coon cats.

Our Core Values

Our commitment to the Main Coon breed is reflected in our core values

❤️

Health First

We prioritize genetic health and physical well-being above all else. Every parent is screened for HCM, SMA and PKDef.

⚖️

Ethical Breeding

We follow strict breeding standards and only plan litters when we are certain we can find the best forever homes.

🤝

Lifetime Support

Our relationship does not end when you take your kiten home. We are always here to offer guidance and support.

🏆

Champion Bloodlines

We work some of the finest Maine Coon lines around the world, importing genetics from Europe and American Catteries.

🏠

Home Environment

our Kittens are raised underfoot in our home, not in cages. They grow up with children, dogs, and everyday house activities.

🩺

Veterinary Partnership

we work closely with our Veterinary team to ensure every kitten receives the best care, vaccinations, and health certification.

Our Commitment to You
